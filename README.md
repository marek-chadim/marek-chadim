### Research

**Markups and Public Procurement: Evidence from Czech Construction Tenders** — First Place, Young Economist of the Year Award, Czech Economic Society (2025). Firms charge markups roughly 15% higher in years they hold government contracts; the premium fell from about 30% in 2006 to 10% in 2021.
