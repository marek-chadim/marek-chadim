## Marek Chadim

Predoctoral Fellow at the Tobin Center for Economic Policy, Yale University.
Website: [marek-chadim.github.io](https://marek-chadim.github.io) · Email: marek.chadim@yale.edu

### Research
**Markups and Public Procurement: Evidence from Czech Construction Tenders**. First Place, Young Economist of the Year Award, Czech Economic Society (2025). Firms charge markups roughly 15% higher in years they hold government contracts; the premium fell from about 30% in 2006 to 10% in 2021. [[paper]](https://marek-chadim.github.io/chadim_markups_procurement.pdf) [[code]](https://github.com/marek-chadim/thesis) [[talk]](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=u0sUwCbN1N8)

Its replication package is part of the [Applied Economics Incubator](https://econincubator.uchicago.edu)'s collection of production-function replication packages (University of Chicago, 2026).
