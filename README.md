## Marek Chadim

Predoctoral Fellow at the Tobin Center for Economic Policy, Yale University, working with Seth Zimmerman and Barbara Biasi.

**Industrial organization** — developing empirical methods for applied microeconomics: demand, production, and the measurement of market power, applied wherever imperfect competition shapes outcomes.

Website: [marek-chadim.github.io](https://marek-chadim.github.io) · Email: marek.chadim@yale.edu

### Research

**Markups and Public Procurement: Evidence from Czech Construction Tenders** — First Place, 32nd Young Economist of the Year Award, Czech Economic Society (2025). Firms charge markups roughly 15% higher in years they hold government contracts; the premium fell from about 30% in 2006 to 10% in 2021. [[paper]](https://marek-chadim.github.io/chadim_markups_procurement.pdf) [[code]](https://github.com/marek-chadim/markups-procurement) [[talk]](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=u0sUwCbN1N8)

**Strong Exclusion for the Production Approach** — research proposal extending Andrews, Barahona, Gentzkow, Rambachan & Shapiro (QJE 2025) to proxy-variable production-function estimation. [[proposal]](https://marek-chadim.github.io/chadim_proposal_strong_exclusion.pdf)
